# Third-party material and attribution

The TadACampaign simulator and evaluated tool implementations originate in the authors' TadA campaign research code. Frozen source lineage and publication transformations are listed in docs/provenance.md and provenance/sources.json. Original packages are the reconstructed offline baseline used by this study, not recovered upstream BioSkill bundles.

Biopython, PyArrow, NumPy, Matplotlib, PyMuPDF, FontTools, svglib, ReportLab and the optional typesafe SDK retain their own licenses. They are installed as dependencies rather than vendored into this repository.

Latin Modern font files are bundled for figure reproduction under the GUST Font License. Preserve paper/assets/fonts/GUST-FONT-LICENSE.txt and its accompanying font notices when redistributing them.

TadA-Bench biological measurements are distributed separately by the upstream project and dataset repository. Their terms remain authoritative. The repository's MIT license does not relicense those measurements, dependency software, agent clients or external model services.

The public manuscript tables and figures retain attribution to the Agent-Native Bioinformatics Tools study. No paper acceptance, DOI or individual author identity is asserted by this software package.
