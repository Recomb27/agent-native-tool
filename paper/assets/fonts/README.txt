The original lmroman10-regular.otf and lmroman10-bold.otf files are Latin
Modern fonts, distributed under the accompanying GUST Font License.

The V13.3 portable Figure 4 renderer converts these original outlines from
CFF OpenType to quadratic TrueType with fontTools, retaining advance widths
and character mappings. Converted fonts are renamed Figure Latin Serif.
They are used for embedded PDF text, not installed as system fonts.
The conversion can be repeated with scripts/font_conversion.py.
